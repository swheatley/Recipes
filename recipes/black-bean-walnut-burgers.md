# Black Bean Walnut Burgers

Meaty, hearty veggie burgers with texture from toasted walnuts. Serves 4. Freezes well.

## Ingredients
- 1 can black beans, drained, rinsed and patted completely dry
- 3/4 cup walnuts, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup sprouted oats
- 1/2 red onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 flax egg (1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water, rested 5 minutes)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil for cooking

## Steps
1. Make the flax egg and let it gel. Pat the beans very dry.
2. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes. Cool slightly.
3. Pulse the beans 4 to 5 times in a food processor into a rough mash. Move to a large bowl.
4. Pulse the walnuts 3 to 4 times and add to the bowl.
5. Add oats, onion, garlic, flax egg, soy sauce and spices. Mix well and adjust seasoning.
6. Form 4 patties about 3/4 inch thick on a parchment lined plate. Chill 15 to 30 minutes.
7. Cook in oil over medium-high heat, 4 to 5 minutes per side without moving them, until they release easily.
8. Serve on buns with guacamole, caramelized onion jam, arugula, tomato and pepperoncini vinaigrette.

## Notes
- Do not over process. You want chunks of bean and walnut, not paste.
- Sauteing the onion first helps if the mixture feels wet.
- Freeze uncooked patties between parchment paper. Make a double batch.
- For comparison, a Live Eat Learn style burger (two cans black beans, bell pepper, panko, egg) came out around 265 calories and 14 g protein per patty.
