# Cajun Smoked Sausage Pepper Jack Pasta

Alex's favorite. Bold, creamy and cheesy, with a vodka rosa sauce. Serves 4. It was a hit with Alex and sits in the regular rotation.

## Ingredients
- 12 oz pasta of choice
- 1 package smoked sausage, sliced into rounds (3 to 4 links is plenty)
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced small
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 jar vodka sauce
- 1/2 cup alfredo sauce
- 1.5 cups shredded pepper jack
- 1/2 cup Mexican cheese blend
- 1/4 cup parmesan, for topping
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Seasoning: 1.5 tsp Slap Ya Mama (replaces the smoked paprika, garlic powder, cayenne and salt), or 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp cayenne, 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

## Steps
1. Cook the pasta al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water.
2. Sear the sausage in the oil over medium-high heat, 2 to 3 minutes per side, until golden. Set aside.
3. In the same pan, cook the onion, carrots, celery and bell pepper 4 to 5 minutes. Add garlic and seasoning for 1 minute.
4. Add the vodka sauce and alfredo. Return the sausage and simmer on medium-low 5 minutes.
5. Add the pasta and toss. Loosen with pasta water if thick.
6. Off the heat, stir in the pepper jack and Mexican blend until melted and creamy.
7. Top with parmesan and serve right away.

## Notes
- Searing the sausage first matters most.
- Add cheese off the heat so it melts smoothly.
- Season in layers. Slap Ya Mama and the sausage are both salty, so taste at the end.
- The formula repeats with other proteins and pasta shapes.
- Leftovers reheat well with a splash of broth or water.
