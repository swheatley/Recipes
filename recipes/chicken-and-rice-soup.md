# Chicken and Rice Soup

Simple, comforting soup with tender vegetables and a savory broth. Serves 6.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb chicken tenderloins or breasts
- 1 cup long grain or jasmine rice, rinsed
- 8 cups chicken broth
- 3 carrots, diced
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 1 red onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley or cilantro, chopped

## Steps
1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high. Cook the onion 3 to 4 minutes, then the garlic 1 minute.
2. Add carrots and celery for 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in thyme, oregano, smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
3. Pour in the broth and bring to a boil. Add the chicken whole. Simmer 20 minutes until cooked through.
4. Remove the chicken and shred with two forks. Return it to the pot.
5. Add the rinsed rice and simmer 18 to 20 minutes until tender.
6. Stir in the lemon juice and herbs. Adjust seasoning.

## Notes
- For meal prep, cook rice separately and add it to each bowl when serving. Rice keeps absorbing broth and can get mushy.
- A parmesan rind in the broth adds depth.
- The soup thickens as it sits. Add broth when reheating.
