# Creamy Spinach Artichoke Chicken Pasta

Dairy free creamy pasta built on a silken tofu sauce. Serves 4. About 520 to 550 calories and 38 to 42 g protein per serving (estimate, with chicken nuggets on top).

## Ingredients
- 12 oz spaghetti or pasta of choice
- 1.5 lb chicken tenderloins or breasts
- 1 block silken tofu (Mori-Nu firm silken works well)
- 1 can artichoke hearts, drained and roughly chopped
- 2 cups fresh or frozen spinach
- 3 tbsp nutritional yeast, plus extra for topping
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 cup reserved pasta water
- 1/4 cup sun dried tomatoes, roughly chopped

## Steps
1. Cook the pasta al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
2. Season the chicken with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Cook in 1 tbsp oil over medium-high heat, 4 to 5 minutes per side. Slice or shred and set aside.
3. Blend the silken tofu until smooth with the nutritional yeast, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt and a splash of pasta water.
4. In the same pan, heat the remaining oil over medium. Cook the garlic 1 minute, then the artichokes and sun dried tomatoes 2 to 3 minutes.
5. Pour in the tofu sauce and stir. Add pasta water a little at a time until creamy and pourable.
6. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Add the pasta and chicken and toss to coat. Adjust seasoning.
7. Serve topped with extra nutritional yeast, sun dried tomatoes and a drizzle of olive oil.

## Notes
- Reserve the pasta water before draining. It sets the sauce consistency.
- Stir in spinach at the end so it stays bright.
- Leftover sauce makes a good dip with crackers or bread.
- Works with chicken nuggets on top.
