# Deli Style Marinated Tofu

Smoky, savory sliced tofu for sandwiches and wraps. Serves 6. Keeps 5 days.

## Ingredients
- 1 block firm tofu, pressed at least 30 minutes, sliced about 1/4 inch thick
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp maple syrup or honey
- 1/2 tsp liquid smoke (optional)
- Pinch of celery seed (optional, very deli flavor)

## Steps
1. Press the tofu and slice as evenly as possible.
2. Whisk the marinade, pour over the slices, and marinate overnight in the fridge (2 hours minimum).
3. Cook in a lightly oiled pan over medium-low heat, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until caramelized and slightly chewy. Or bake at 375°F for 20 to 25 minutes, flipping halfway.
4. Cool completely, store airtight, and serve cold.

## Notes
- High heat makes it rubbery. Low and slow gives the chewy deli texture.
- Sandwich idea: bread, tofu slices, pepperoncini, roasted red pepper, arugula, cucumber, pepperoncini vinaigrette spread.
