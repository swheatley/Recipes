# Green Lentil Spread

Creamy, protein packed spread for sandwiches, toast or as a veggie dip. Keeps 5 days in a sealed jar. Serves 8.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup green lentils, rinsed
- 2.5 cups water or vegetable broth
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast (optional)
- 2 tbsp tahini (optional)

## Steps
1. Simmer lentils in the water or broth for 25 to 30 minutes, until very soft. Drain any excess liquid.
2. Blend with the remaining ingredients until smooth. Add a splash of water if thick.
3. Adjust seasoning and store in a sealed jar in the fridge.

## Notes
- First batch came out a little bitter (green lentils run more bitter than red). Fixes: more lemon, a little honey or maple syrup, more olive oil, a pinch of salt, extra smoked paprika.
- Good on rye, sourdough, pita or lentil tortillas, with cucumber, tomato, avocado, sprouts or shredded carrots.
