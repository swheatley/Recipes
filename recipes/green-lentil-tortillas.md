# Green Lentil Tortillas

Flour free, high protein wraps made from blended soaked lentils. Makes about 8.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup green lentils, soaked 4 to 8 hours and drained
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp olive oil

## Steps
1. Blend everything for 1 to 2 minutes until completely smooth. The batter should pour like crepe batter. Add water a tablespoon at a time if thick.
2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-high with no oil. Pour about 1/4 cup batter in the center and swirl thin.
3. Cook 2 to 3 minutes until the edges look dry and the surface is set. Flip and cook 1 more minute.
4. Stack under a clean towel and cool completely before storing.

## Notes
- Overnight soak is best.
- The first tortilla is a test one. Too low a heat and they will not set.
- Store between parchment in an airtight container up to 5 days. Reheat in a dry pan 30 seconds per side.
