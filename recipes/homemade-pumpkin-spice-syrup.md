# Homemade Pumpkin Spice Syrup

For coffee, lattes, pancakes, oatmeal and yogurt. Ready in about 10 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup water
- 1 cup sugar (brown sugar gives a deeper caramel flavor)
- 3 tbsp canned pumpkin puree
- 1.5 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp cloves or allspice
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Steps
1. Combine water and sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
2. Add the pumpkin, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cloves and salt. Whisk until smooth.
3. Bring to a gentle simmer, stirring often, and cook 5 minutes until slightly thickened. Do not boil hard.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the vanilla. Cool 10 minutes.
5. Strain through a fine mesh strainer into a clean jar. Cool completely before refrigerating.

## Notes
- Use 1 to 2 tbsp per cup of coffee. A splash of condensed milk makes a pumpkin spice latte.
- Keeps up to 2 weeks in the fridge. Shake or stir before using.
- Also good on baked apple oatmeal.
