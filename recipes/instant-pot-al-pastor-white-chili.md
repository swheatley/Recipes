# Instant Pot Al Pastor White Chili

Creamy white chili with heirloom ayocote blanco beans and al pastor pork. Serves 6. Alex loves white chicken chili, and this is a version of it.

## Ingredients
- 1 cup dry ayocote blanco beans, soaked and drained
- 1.5 lb al pastor pork, chunky (cooked or raw, see notes)
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 red onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cans green chiles (4 oz each)
- 1 cup frozen corn
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 4 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped

## Steps
1. Press Saute. Heat the oil and cook the onion 3 to 4 minutes. Add garlic for 1 minute.
2. Add cumin, oregano, chili powder and smoked paprika and toast 1 minute. Press Cancel.
3. Add the soaked beans, green chiles, pork, salt and broth. Stir. Do not fill past the max line.
4. Seal and pressure cook on High for 25 minutes.
5. Natural release 15 minutes, then quick release the rest.
6. Press Saute. Mash about 1 cup of beans against the side of the pot and stir back in to thicken.
7. Stir in the cream cheese until melted. Stir in the corn (residual heat cooks it) and the lime juice. Adjust seasoning.
8. Top with cilantro, extra lime, sour cream, shredded cheese or jalapeños.

## Notes
- Used 4 cups broth because the Instant Pot retains liquid.
- Raw al pastor also works. Raise the pressure cook time to 35 to 40 minutes and skim excess fat before adding the cream cheese.
- Costco al pastor is well seasoned. Taste before adding more salt, and consider holding back about a quarter of the pork.
- Thickens as it sits, so add broth if needed. Better the next day.
