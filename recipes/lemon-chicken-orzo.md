# Lemon Chicken Orzo

One pan comfort meal with chicken, lemony broth and creamy orzo. Serves 4.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs
- 1.5 cups orzo
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 lemon, juiced and zested (second lemon in wedges on the side)
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

## Steps
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and Italian seasoning. Sear in olive oil over medium-high heat, 4 to 5 minutes per side. Set aside.
2. Reduce to medium, add butter and onion for 2 to 3 minutes, then garlic for 1 minute.
3. Add orzo and toast 1 to 2 minutes.
4. Add broth, lemon juice and zest. Nestle the chicken back in, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 12 to 15 minutes.
5. Remove the chicken and shred or slice it. Stir in spinach until wilted, fold in parmesan, return the chicken, and adjust seasoning.

## Notes
- Our first batch used two whole lemons and was too tart. Use one lemon and serve wedges.
- If it is too sharp: add broth, butter, parmesan or a pinch of sugar.
- Thighs stay juicier. Kale works in place of spinach.
- Freezes well without the spinach. Add fresh spinach when reheating.
