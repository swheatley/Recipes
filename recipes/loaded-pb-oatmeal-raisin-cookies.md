# Loaded PB Oatmeal Raisin Cookies

A hearty travel cookie, even better on day two. Makes about 24.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups sprouted oats
- 1 cup all purpose flour
- 3/4 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp flax seed
- 3/4 cup raisins
- 1/3 cup walnuts, roughly chopped
- 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
- 1/4 cup unsweetened shredded coconut
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Steps
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
2. Beat butter, peanut butter, brown sugar and granulated sugar until light and fluffy, 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Mix in eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
4. Stir in flour, baking soda, cinnamon, salt and flax seed until just combined.
5. Fold in oats, raisins, walnuts, pumpkin seeds and coconut.
6. Scoop heaping tablespoons 2 inches apart and flatten slightly. Bake 11 to 13 minutes, until the edges are set and the centers look barely underdone.
7. Cool on the sheet for 5 minutes, then move to a rack.

## Notes
- Keep total mix-ins to about 2 cups or the dough will not hold together.
- Store airtight for up to 5 days.
