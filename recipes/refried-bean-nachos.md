# Refried Bean Nachos

## Steps
1. Spread chips in a single layer, slightly overlapping, on a sheet pan.
2. Warm the refried beans so they spread easily. Layer beans, a melty cheese (Monterey Jack, cheddar or a Mexican blend), pickled jalapeños and diced red onion. Layer again for extra cheese coverage.
3. Bake at 400°F for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese is melted. Do not overbake or the chips turn bitter.
4. Top with sour cream, salsa, cilantro, avocado or guacamole and a squeeze of lime.

## Notes
- Hot sauce or chipotle crema on top works well.
- Tostadas are a fast variation (under 30 minutes).
