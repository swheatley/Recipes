# Spaghetti Pasta Salad with Ranch (Nugget Noodles)

Creamy summer pasta salad with nuggets and bacon on top. Serves 4. Kid friendly if you keep the cabbage on the side and serve the components separately.

## Ingredients
- 1 box spaghetti, cooked and cooled
- 12 chicken nuggets, cooked and roughly chopped
- 6 strips bacon, cooked and crumbled
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup frozen corn, thawed
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 red onion, diced small
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 3/4 cup ranch dressing (a ranch cottage cheese dressing also worked)
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar or lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Optional add-ins: shredded carrot (about 1/2 to 1 cup), shredded cheddar, red cabbage on the side

## Steps
1. Cook the spaghetti al dente, rinse in cold water, toss with a little olive oil, and chill.
2. Cook the bacon until crisp and crumble. Cook the nuggets and chop. Keep both separate.
3. Whisk the ranch with the vinegar, garlic powder, smoked paprika and pepper.
4. Combine the spaghetti, tomatoes, corn, black beans, red onion and green onions. Pour the dressing over and toss.
5. Refrigerate 20 to 30 minutes. Top with the nuggets and bacon right before serving.

## Notes
- Add nuggets and bacon at the last minute so they stay crisp.
- Red cabbage bleeds purple into the ranch. Serve it on the side, which also helps with a daughter who thought the purple version looked gross.
- For the 6 year old: plain spaghetti with ranch, nuggets and bacon, corn on the side, or let her build her own bowl. Calling it "nugget noodles" helps.
- Rinse the thawed corn under cold water.
