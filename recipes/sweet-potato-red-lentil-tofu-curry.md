# Sweet Potato Red Lentil & Tofu Curry

Rich, creamy tomato-coconut curry with sweet potato, melty red lentils and crispy air fried tofu. Medium heat. Serves 4.

## Ingredients
- 1 block extra firm tofu, air fried and cubed
- 1 large sweet potato, peeled and cubed
- 3/4 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 1 red onion, diced
- 1 can full fat coconut milk
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp olive or coconut oil
- 1.5 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup water or vegetable broth

## Steps
1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion 4 to 5 minutes until softened and starting to caramelize.
2. Add garlic and ginger for 1 to 2 minutes. Add curry powder, garam masala, turmeric, cumin and cayenne. Toast the spices for 1 minute.
3. Add the diced tomatoes, sweet potato and lentils. Stir together.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and water or broth. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered 20 to 25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sweet potato is tender and the lentils have melted into the sauce.
5. Adjust salt and spice. Fold in the air fried tofu just before serving. Serve over rice or with naan.

## Notes
- The lentils thicken the curry. If it gets too thick, add broth or water.
- A squeeze of lemon or lime at the end brightens it.
- Freezing: freeze the curry base without the tofu, then air fry fresh tofu when reheating. If the tofu is already mixed in, it still freezes fine but turns softer and chewier. Keeps up to 3 months. Add a splash of broth when reheating.
- Crumbled crispy shiitake mushrooms make a good topper.
