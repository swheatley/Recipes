# Tofu Bacon

Smoky, sweet, savory strips made from frozen then thawed super firm tofu. Great in a BLT wrap with cream cheese, lettuce and red onion.

## Marinade
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp liquid smoke (optional)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Pinch of black pepper

## Steps
1. Press the thawed tofu well and slice into 1/8 inch strips.
2. Whisk the marinade and coat the tofu. Rest 15 to 20 minutes, longer if you have time.
3. Pan fry on medium-high in a lightly oiled pan, a few minutes per side, until caramelized and crisp at the edges. Or bake at 400°F for 20 to 25 minutes, flipping halfway.
4. Brush on a little extra maple syrup in the last minute for caramelization.

## Notes
- No liquid smoke: use Worcestershire, a little minced chipotle in adobo, or increase the smoked paprika.
- Strips crisp up more as they cool.
- Not for dogs. The soy sauce and garlic powder are unsafe for them.
