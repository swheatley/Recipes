# Tuna Casserole (Goodles)

Alex's favorite comfort meal. One box of Goodles feeds 3 with 1 or 2 servings of leftovers.

## Ingredients
- 1 box Goodles sheep's milk mac and cheese (cook noodles just shy of al dente)
- Canned tuna
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup, thinned with a splash of milk
- Cream cheese, softened, mixed into the soup base
- Peas
- Diced red onion
- Crushed Ritz crackers with butter for the topping
- Shredded cheese for the top (optional)

## Steps
1. Mix tuna, soup, softened cream cheese, peas and red onion.
2. Fold in the slightly undercooked noodles. Loosen with milk or pasta water if thick.
3. Top with buttered cracker crumbs and cheese.
4. Bake at 375°F for 25 to 30 minutes, until bubbly and golden.
